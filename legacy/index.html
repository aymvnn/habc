How we work

Five steps, in this order, every time.

Technology narrows the search; people verify.

  1. 1

    Tell us what you need

    A short profile: what you offer, what you look for, in which market. We do not ask for your CV; we ask for your commercial need, precisely.

  2. 2

    We verify and admit

    Company registration, activity, references. Not everyone is admitted; that is what makes membership worth having. Card and membership number are issued on admission.

  3. 3

    We search and match

    Inside and beyond the network. Technology narrows the search, but a person reviews every match before it reaches you. We do not send lists.

  4. 4

    We introduce you properly

    A written brief to both sides. We join the first call where needed and translate the cultural gap before it becomes a misunderstanding.

  5. 5

    We stay until the first deal

    Documents, certificates, freight, customs, payment terms — until the first shipment lands or the first contract is signed. After that, the relationship is yours.

Why DUKAAT

Three things are ours alone.

A search engine gives you names. It does not build the relationship.

I

We are traders, not consultants.

DUKAAT was founded by someone who imports, packs and ships goods himself — not by an advisory firm that has never handled a container.

II

We sit next to Rotterdam, Europe’s largest port.

The gateway through which goods enter Europe is our neighbourhood, not a reference on a map. One hour from the market our members want to reach.

III

We are trusted on both sides, in both languages.

Our network did not start with a database. It started with people who already knew us — in Arabic and in Dutch.

Members’ current requests circulate inside the network, verified and matched by a person. They are not published on this page.

The name

A coin both sides already knew.

Since 4 August 1586 the Dutch gold ducat has been a trade coin under the Dutch Mint Act: 3.494 grams of gold at 983/1000, and the Royal Dutch Mint in Utrecht still strikes it today. Its strength was not the gold alone. A merchant who saw it knew exactly what it represented. The Ottoman sultani of 1477–78 was struck on the ducat standard; Moroccan gold dinars were called bunduqi, after Venice, because the coin had travelled that far.

The ducat was a language both sides already spoke.

Membership

Membership with defined commitments.

Three levels — Member, Professional and Executive — each with commitments we put in writing: how quickly we reply, what we owe you in introductions and searches, and what happens if we fall short. Founding 100 is a status held next to them: a permanent number, the founding rate, a name on the founders’ wall, the right to nominate.

Membership is an annual subscription. Project services (market entry, supplier search, representation) are quoted per engagement in writing. No hidden commissions.

Membership is by nomination. There is no application button on this site, and there will not be one.

Founder · Trader

DUKAAT did not start as a business plan. It started as a problem I kept running into. I run a contract-packing business in the Netherlands, legumes and nuts; I buy in the Arab world and sell into Europe. Again and again: a factory that could deliver exactly what a buyer needed, and a buyer who would never find it. I made those introductions as a favour for years. DUKAAT is doing it properly.

[Founder 2 — fiscal law]

Founder · Fiscal law

A good introduction fails on the paperwork more often than on the people. My side of DUKAAT is the structure: which entity signs, what the contract says, how the tax and customs position works out, and whether the other party is who they say they are. Before any member meets a counterparty, someone here has checked the registration and read the documents. That is not caution for its own sake. It is what makes the handshake worth something.
