# 2. Get to know DUKAAT — detailed content

## 2.1 About us

**Title:** A space where relationships meet knowledge and opportunities

DUKAAT is a business club and platform that connects companies, entrepreneurs, investors, manufacturers, suppliers, buyers and experts between the Netherlands and the Arab world. We are building a community that helps its members get to know one another, exchange expertise, understand markets and develop opportunities for commercial and professional cooperation.

You may be a company looking for a distributor in a new market, an importer in need of a suitable supplier, or the owner of a venture looking for a partner. Or you may be a professional looking to develop your expertise, or a student preparing for the start of your working life. At DUKAAT, we begin by understanding what you need and what you can offer, and then help you choose the right path.

The DUKAAT ecosystem brings together business connections, training, meetings, consultancy and the showcasing of products and opportunities. We believe that a good relationship grows when clarity comes together with knowledge and mutual interest, and that cooperation between the Netherlands and the Arab world holds value in both directions.

**Subtitle: Why DUKAAT?**

Because finding a company's name is only the beginning of the search. After that, you need to understand how well it fits, know what you are looking for, and know how to begin a useful conversation with it. DUKAAT helps you structure your needs, develop your knowledge and build relationships tied to a clear goal.

**Buttons:** Discover our services | Learn about membership

## 2.2 The story behind the name

**Title:** From value that crosses markets to relationships that cross borders

The name DUKAAT draws its inspiration from the Dutch ducat and its trading heritage: a symbol we reinterpret today through the value of people, their knowledge and their relationships.

Value may be expertise shared by a specialist, a product that finds its market, or an idea that meets someone who helps it grow. And it may begin with a meeting that brings together two people who were each looking for the other.

This is where the DUKAAT story comes from: a community that carries value from person to person, from company to company and from market to market. At its heart are three values: value, trust and leadership.

**Subtitle: Every member carries the meaning of nobility**

In DUKAAT's culture, we use ‘the Duke’ as a symbol of noble conduct and responsible leadership: someone who keeps their word, respects others, shares their knowledge and opens a door to a new opportunity. It is a symbolic identity of belonging within the club, not a formal aristocratic title.

**Closing line:** At DUKAAT, a member's nobility shows in the mark they leave: knowledge they share, a pledge they keep and an opportunity they open.

## 2.3 Our vision

**Title:** A business community that opens horizons for cooperation across borders

For DUKAAT to become a point of reference for business and knowledge between the Netherlands and the Arab world, and a community that people with ambition turn to when they are looking for a partner, a market, expertise or an opportunity to grow.

We aspire to build long-term relationships that make cooperation between companies and individuals clearer and more effective, and that allow expertise, products and ideas to reach those who need them, with scope to extend this connection to Europe and other markets.

## 2.4 Our mission

**Title:** Turning relationships into value and business opportunities

We connect companies and entrepreneurs with knowledge, partners and opportunities between the Netherlands and the Arab world, through guided professional introductions, practical training, specialised events, and information and services that support taking the next step.

We start from a clear need and help turn it into a structured search, and then into dialogue and cooperation that can be built upon.

## 2.5 Our goals

- **Connecting the business community:** bringing companies, entrepreneurs and experts together around shared needs and interests.
- **Exchanging knowledge:** offering courses, workshops and meetings that turn expertise into practical application.
- **Facilitating market entry:** helping companies understand the target market and prepare to engage with it.
- **Growing trade:** supporting introductions in supply, purchasing, distribution, import, export and manufacturing.
- **Showcasing products and opportunities:** providing an organised space for trade offers, purchase requests, clearance stock and equipment.
- **Supporting investment connections:** enabling introductions between project owners and those interested in investing.
- **Developing professional skills:** serving professionals, university students and those about to enter the job market.
- **Strengthening trust:** encouraging clear information and respect for commitments, privacy and mutual interests.
- **Organising purposeful meetings:** linking workshops, exhibitions and delegations to the needs of participants.

**Closing button:** Tell us what you want to achieve

# 3. Connecting companies and entrepreneurs

**Supporting name:** DUKAAT Connect

**Title:** Finding the right party starts with understanding your needs

**Introduction:** We help companies and entrepreneurs reach potential suppliers, buyers, distributors and partners, based on the nature of their activity, the market and the commercial objective.

Start with two questions: What do I offer? And what am I looking for? Whether you are offering a product or a service, or looking for supply, distribution or collaboration, a clear answer helps direct the search and introductions towards the parties closest to your needs.

## Who is this service for?

- A Dutch company looking for a distributor or importer in an Arab country.
- An Arab company preparing to reach buyers in the Netherlands and Europe.
- An importer who needs a manufacturer or supplier meeting specific specifications.
- A business owner looking for a commercial, technical or operational partner.
- A company looking for manufacturing under its own brand, or a new sales channel.

## How do we work?

1. **We understand the request:** the product or service, the sector, the country and the required specifications.
2. **We define the scope of assistance:** what does the request need, and which service suits it?
3. **We look for the fit:** we review the available parties and information against the agreed criteria.
4. **We prepare the introduction:** where there is mutual interest, we help initiate contact with the consent of both parties.
5. **We arrange the next step:** an introductory meeting, an exchange of information or a request for a quotation, depending on the case.

## What makes a request stronger?

Provide a clear description of what you need, the quantity or volume of work, the target country, the expected timing and what sets your offer apart. The more precise the information, the easier it becomes to assess the fit.

**Service clarification:** An introduction is a step towards exploring collaboration; concluding an agreement depends on both parties and their assessment of the terms and of the capacity to deliver. The scope of the search and follow-up is determined before the service begins.

**Buttons:** Submit a business connection request | Add what your company offers

# 4. Academy and training courses

**Supporting name:** DUKAAT Academy

**Title:** Knowledge that turns into skill, and skill that opens an opportunity for you

The Academy is one of the core pillars of DUKAAT. Through it, we develop courses, lectures and workshops led by specialists and experienced practitioners, to connect knowledge with the needs of work and professional life.

We address entrepreneurs and company owners; professionals such as lawyers, doctors, engineers and others; university students and graduates; and anyone who wants to enter the labour market or develop their career path. The programme varies with its participants, and the aim remains the same: learning you can use.

## How do we learn at DUKAAT?

Our methodology draws inspiration from Dutch and European practices in applied education: dialogue, the study of real-life cases, group work, problem-solving, simulation and receiving feedback. Participants take part by asking questions, trying things out and discussing, and work on practical exercises linked to the programme’s objectives.

You may leave a workshop with a plan for presenting a project, a purchase request template, a clearer way of negotiating, or steps to improve your professional presence. On each programme’s page, we set out the skills it targets and what participants will work on.

## Academy tracks

### Entrepreneurship and business management

From idea to business model: understanding the customer, formulating the value proposition, planning, pricing, managing operations and the team, and presenting the venture to partners and customers.

### International trade and market entry

Import and export, selecting suppliers and distributors, procurement, delivery terms, payment methods, logistics, and the culture of negotiation between the Netherlands and the Arab world. Specialised programmes address the requirements of the target market, in collaboration with specialists in the subject.

### Marketing, sales and artificial intelligence

Developing brand identity and the commercial offer, business-to-business sales, digital marketing, building relationships through professional platforms, and using AI tools for research, content, customer service and organising work.

### Financial management and understanding investment

Reading financial statements, managing cash flow, calculating costs, understanding feasibility studies, assessing assumptions and risks, and preparing questions before taking a business or investment decision.

### Professional development for specialists

Programmes suited to lawyers, doctors, engineers and all other professionals, covering the management of offices, clinics and projects, client communication, leadership, digital technologies and collaboration across disciplines. Each programme clearly sets out its subject, its requirements and its level.

### Students, graduates and preparing for the labour market

The CV, the interview, introducing yourself professionally, working in a team, presenting and communicating, understanding the working environment, and turning university knowledge into applicable skills.

## Forms of learning

- **Introductory lectures:** a clear way into a specific topic, with direct questions to the speaker.
- **Specialised courses:** a step-by-step track to develop a skill or a set of skills.
- **Group workshops:** practical exercises and challenges that participants work on in teams.
- **Meetings with experts:** discussion of practical questions related to a specialism or a market.
- **Advanced and executive sessions:** deeper topics for experienced professionals and managers.
- **Dedicated training for companies:** a programme designed around the team’s needs and the organisation’s goals.
- **In-person or remote learning:** depending on the nature of the programme and where participants are based.

## Knowledge that is accessible for starting out, and specialised for growth

We are working to develop free or subsidised initiatives and introductory lectures, particularly for students and those about to enter working life, alongside paid specialised courses and programmes for companies. The type of participation and its fees, if any, are stated on each programme’s page.

We seek to collaborate with universities, institutions and professional bodies to make wider learning opportunities available; the names of participating organisations are announced once the collaboration with them is confirmed.

## Are you a trainer or an expert?

If you have practical experience and can turn it into a useful learning experience, share with us your field of specialisation, your training idea and the group you would like to serve.

# 5. Workshops, events and exhibitions

**Supporting name:** DUKAAT Events

**Title:** A meeting with a purpose, and a dialogue to build on

We bring together people with related interests in gatherings that help them get to know one another, learn and explore opportunities for collaboration. We start from a question: who needs to meet whom, and why? Then we build an experience suited to the event’s theme and its participants.

## Group workshops

A space to work on a shared challenge: developing an idea, solving a problem or improving a commercial offer. Participants exchange perspectives, apply tools, present their results and receive feedback from the facilitator and the group.

## Business meetings and matchmaking

Targeted meetings between companies, in which each participant explains what they offer and what they are looking for. They may include short one-to-one meetings, sector sessions, or a meeting with a buyer or supplier around a specific need.

## Expert meetings and executive round tables

Specialised conversations about markets, sectors and management challenges. Sessions with limited places offer room for deeper discussion and the exchange of experience, under the participation conditions announced for each meeting.

## Exhibitions and conferences

Opportunities to get to know products, services and companies, and to build a presence in target markets. Programmes may include group participation or a joint stand for companies, when announced, with a clear explanation of what the arrangements include and what participation requires.

## How do you choose the right event?

Review the topic, the participating groups, the programme, the language and the venue. Then define your goal in attending and what you would like to offer or discuss, so that the meeting is more useful for you and for others.

# 6. Business services and consultancy

**Supporting name:** DUKAAT Advisory

**Title:** Greater clarity for the next step in your business

You may need to understand a market, organise a commercial request, look for a partner or reach a specialist. We help you define your need and direct it to the right service, clarifying the scope and deliverables before work begins.

## Market entry

Identifying the market and potential customers, understanding sales channels, and preparing the questions and information needed to get started. An initial plan of action can be prepared, or a broader study coordinated, depending on the size of the project.

## Finding suppliers and manufacturers

Drafting specifications and selection criteria, and carrying out an initial search for sources of supply or manufacturing, including private-label manufacturing, raw materials and equipment.

## Finding distributors and partners

Defining the type of party sought, the country and the sector, and preparing the cooperation proposal; then researching potential parties and paving the way for contact when both fit and interest are present.

## Developing the commercial presentation and marketing

Improving the company introduction, the product presentation and sales messages, preparing for meetings and exhibitions, and thinking through how to reach customers in the target market.

## Specialised consultancy

When a legal, tax, financial or technical opinion is needed, we work to coordinate access to a suitable specialist, depending on the country, the subject and the availability of the service. The proposal states the name of the service provider, the scope of their work and their fees.

## Coordinating business requests

Tell us what you want to achieve, and we will help you structure the request and determine the priorities and the right path. This may include preparing for the meeting, organising information, and coordinating introductions and follow-up within an agreed scope.

**How do you start?** Send a brief description of your project, the target country, and the question or outcome you need. We review the request, then clarify the proposed service and its details.

# 20. Frequently asked questions

## Is DUKAAT only for companies?

DUKAAT speaks to companies, entrepreneurs, professionals and investors, and offers knowledge programmes suited to students, graduates and those about to start work. Conditions for participation vary by programme and service.

## Can I take part without membership?

The page for each event or programme states whether participation is open to everyone or reserved for members. You can also send an enquiry or a service request to find out which options suit you.

## Does membership include all courses and services?

The benefits included are set out in the membership offer. Some courses, consultancy services, delegations and extended research assignments may be separate services.

## How does DUKAAT help me find a partner or supplier?

We start by understanding the request and defining the search criteria and the scope of the service, then we look for potential parties and arrange an introduction where there is a fit and mutual interest.

## Does DUKAAT guarantee that deals are completed?

We help with the search, introductions and preparing for cooperation. Evaluation, negotiation, agreement and execution remain the responsibility of the parties, in accordance with the terms of their dealings.

## Are all courses free?

Free or subsidised lectures or initiatives may be available alongside paid programmes. Fees and conditions of participation are stated on each programme's page.

## Does a participant receive a certificate?

The programme page states whether a certificate of attendance or participation is available, the issuing body and the conditions for obtaining it. This does not imply academic or professional accreditation unless a specific accreditation is stated.

## How do I list a product or a purchase request?

Use the form for submitting an offer or a purchase request, and add the necessary details and attachments. The information is reviewed before it is published or shared, in line with the service available.

## What languages are the website and programmes in?

The website offers Arabic, English and Dutch. The language of each programme or service is specified in its details; you can state your preferred language of communication in your request.
