## Design Context

### Users
Owners and decision-makers of Dutch/European manufacturers, traders and service firms looking for distributors, suppliers, buyers or market entry in the Arab world; and Arab investors, manufacturers and importers looking for the right European counterpart. They arrive because someone nominated them or because an approached company googles DUKAAT to pass a credibility check. Context: desk, daytime, serious money on the table; both cultures buy from a person, not an entity. Trilingual audience: English (default), Dutch, Arabic (RTL).

### Brand Personality
Entrepreneurs AND advisers, the complete picture (owner, 30 Sep 2026: "verstand van zaken zonder stoer te doen"; 1 Oct 2026: never set one against the other — "entrepreneurs, not consultants" was dismissive of advice and is gone). Precise · trusted · quietly grand. Understated confidence: mechanism over promises, no salesmanship, no hype. Warm (paper, gold, bordeaux) but disciplined. The ducat is the metaphor: a known value, a trusted weight, a standard both sides already spoke.

### Aesthetic Direction
Bold & groots on a light canvas: bone/linen paper ground, bordeaux (#6F0A25) and gold (#DCB17A→#9E7946, bar #B39455) taken from the logo, large confident typography, generous space, at most one dark block. The tulip mark with crossing gold ribbons and the shared gold A-bar are the signature motifs. Anti-references: the previous dark heraldic "holding" site ("te ouderwets, te donker, typografie niet mooi"), Alibaba-style marketplaces, chamber-of-commerce templates, any AI-slop tells. Light theme only.

### Design Principles
1. The logo is leading — every colour, curve and motif on the site is earned from the SVG, never invented.
2. Density with rhythm, not emptiness — the owner (1 Oct 2026) found the site "far too much white space, boring layouts, like a standard template": fewer elements but larger and closer together, text set for the width it owns, photographs and text composed as one piece; no card grids, no fake numbers, no filler. Modern and technically ahead, high-class without being old-fashioned; real, connected effects (one light, one gold line) over scattered tricks.
3. The compagnon's text is the master — every word on the site comes from owntext/ (Arabic verbatim, NL/EN translated); design never rewrites copy, it only sets it.
4. A club, not a marketplace — visitors first get to know DUKAAT; nothing is public for now (content/visibility.ts); no technology/AI claims.
5. Three languages, one house — RTL and Arabic type are first-class, not an afterthought.
6. One header for every inner page — page name (--h1), the source's title line as a serif standfirst under a short gold bar, the photograph as a 4:3 plate; section headings on one step (--h2), items on --h3; headings weight 700.
