# Test: reportage met mensen, realistischer

Proef voor drukke, echte situaties (beurs, warenhuis, conferentie). Zelfde licht- en kleurzinnen als het stijlboek; nieuw is een documentaire aanpak met mensen van achteren en op afstand, en echte, licht onperfecte details. Eén plek per beeld: Nederland óf de Arabische wereld, nooit twee skylines in één raam.

### beurs-buiten

**Formaat:** 16:9 · **Licht:** Helder hedendaags (buiten) · **Wereld:** Nederland

```
Create a photorealistic editorial photograph. A wide documentary view at standing eye level with a 35mm lens of the entrance of a large modern exhibition hall on an overcast Dutch morning, a long low facade of glass and dark steel with plain, unmarked banners, wet grey paving stones after a light shower and a row of parked black bicycles along the edge of the square. A few visitors in dark wool coats walk toward the glass doors with leather folders under their arms, small and slightly soft in the middle distance, all seen from behind, their faces turned away and out of view, while a flat polder horizon with a thin line of bare trees runs behind the hall. Real, lived-in details: puddles reflecting the facade, a bicycle bell catching the light, a folded umbrella hooked on a railing. Bright, soft, diffused daylight through a high, pale haze lies evenly over the scene, with clean whites, gentle short shadows and a light haze softening the distance. Natural, rich colour with a restrained grade: softly lifted blacks, warm honey midtones, highlights held at a soft warm white, very fine grain, every colour coming from the real materials and the light. Any writing stays soft and unreadable, and every object is unbranded. Calm editorial composition with generous negative space and a natural, gently shallow depth of field. Photorealistic, shot on a medium-format camera, with the honest look of a real photograph. Aspect ratio 16:9
```
