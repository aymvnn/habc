# DUKAAT — Missie, nieuwe richting (voorbeelden)

14 oktober 2026. De eigenaar keurde de werksessie voor Missie af: "bij missie willen we juist een foto van ver, van een fleet vrachtwagens; dit moet laten zien dat de vrachtwagens allemaal gecoördineerd een route hebben en dat wij ook verbanden kunnen leggen voor de automotive wereld".

Drie voorbeelden in lage kwaliteit: `npm run photos -- --sheet brand/fotostijl/missie-fotos.md --preview --n 1`. Na zijn keuze komt er één definitief op 2K met Pro (id `f-mission`).

---

### missie-a

**Formaat:** 21:9 · **Licht:** gouden namiddag · **Idee:** de vloot volgt één route door de haven

```
Create a photorealistic aerial editorial photograph, one single continuous frame. A high drone view, looking down at an angle from about two hundred metres, over a wide motorway that curves through the port area of a great Dutch port city in late-afternoon light. A coordinated fleet of fourteen identical modern trucks drives in a single, evenly spaced convoy along the right-hand lane, all with deep oxblood-red cabs and plain silver-white trailers, following one route toward the container terminals, where rows of tall quay cranes stand above stacks of plain, unmarked containers in muted colours. The other lanes carry a little ordinary traffic in realistic colours, a railway line and a canal run beside the motorway, and in the far distance the wide river, the city towers and a white cable-stayed bridge with one tall angled pylon lie in a warm haze. The trucks carry no logos, words or numbers, and their number plates are too small to read. Long, soft shadows of the trucks fall across the asphalt. Warm, golden late-afternoon sunlight from the side, a clear sky with a light haze over the horizon. True-to-life colour with warm, rich tones, very fine natural grain, crisp detail and no HDR or CGI look; it must look like a real aerial photograph. The convoy runs through the middle of the frame so that a central 3:2 crop still shows it. The photograph fills the whole 21:9 frame edge to edge with no border. Aspect ratio 21:9
```

### missie-b

**Formaat:** 21:9 · **Licht:** blauw uur · **Idee:** verbanden leggen: een knooppunt waar routes samenkomen

```
Create a photorealistic aerial editorial photograph, one single continuous frame. A high drone view, looking almost straight down at a slight angle, over a large motorway interchange outside a Dutch port city at the blue hour, its curving ramps and flyovers forming an elegant pattern of connected routes. A coordinated fleet of twelve identical modern trucks, all with deep oxblood-red cabs and plain silver-white trailers, flows in one evenly spaced convoy from one motorway onto another along a sweeping ramp, their headlights and tail lights on, while ordinary evening traffic in realistic colours moves on the other roads. Around the interchange lie flat green polder fields with ditches, a few warehouses and, on the horizon, the soft lights of the port cranes and the city. The trucks carry no logos, words or numbers, and their number plates are too small to read. The light of the blue hour just after sunset: the sky a deep, even blue fading to warm orange at the horizon, the road lights glowing amber, calm and low in contrast. True-to-life colour, very fine natural grain, crisp detail and no HDR, light trails or CGI look; it must look like a real aerial photograph. The convoy on its ramp sits in the middle of the frame so that a central 3:2 crop still shows it. The photograph fills the whole 21:9 frame edge to edge with no border. Aspect ratio 21:9
```

### missie-c

**Formaat:** 21:9 · **Licht:** heldere ochtend · **Idee:** de automotive-wereld: nieuwe auto's in colonne naar het autoschip

```
Create a photorealistic aerial editorial photograph, one single continuous frame. A high drone view, looking down at an angle, over the vehicle terminal of a great Dutch port on a clear morning. A coordinated convoy of ten identical car-transporter trucks with deep oxblood-red cabs, each loaded with new cars in realistic colours, drives in an evenly spaced line along the terminal road toward a large car-carrier ship moored at the quay, its high plain hull and open stern ramp ready for loading. Beside the road, wide parking fields hold long, neat rows of new cars waiting for export, and a few terminal staff in high-visibility jackets are tiny figures between them. Beyond the quay lie the wide river, quay cranes and the distant city towers in a light morning haze. The trucks, cars and ship carry no logos, words or numbers, and number plates are too small to read. Clear, low morning sunlight from one side, with long soft shadows and a fresh, bright atmosphere. True-to-life colour with warm, rich tones, very fine natural grain, crisp detail and no HDR or CGI look; it must look like a real aerial photograph. The convoy runs through the middle of the frame so that a central 3:2 crop still shows it. The photograph fills the whole 21:9 frame edge to edge with no border. Aspect ratio 21:9
```
