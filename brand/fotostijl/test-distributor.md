# Test: de trekker buiten op het land

De distributeursfoto toonde een trekker in een showroom; een trekker hoort op het land. Twee versies, beide volgens het stijlboek (sector landbouwmachines, werkplek buiten).

### distributor-polder

**Formaat:** 3:2 · **Licht:** Helder hedendaags (buiten, na de regen) · **Wereld:** Nederland

```
Create a photorealistic editorial photograph. A wide view at standing eye level with a 35mm lens across a vast flat green polder meadow: a new unbadged tractor in deep red paint stands on the right third on a narrow farm track at the edge of the field, its steel plates plain and its tyres carrying fresh mud. Lush grass runs toward a straight drainage ditch and a row of tall poplars, and a low horizon with a distant farmhouse lies under a wide sky. Composed with a little calm space above and below the subject. Soft, even daylight falls from a low grey sky just after rain, the paving and every surface wet and softly reflective, the air clear and cool, with gentle short shadows. Natural, rich colour with a restrained grade: softly lifted blacks, warm honey midtones, highlights held at a soft warm white, very fine grain, every colour coming from the real materials and the light. Any writing stays soft and unreadable, and every object is unbranded. Calm editorial composition with generous negative space and a natural, gently shallow depth of field. Photorealistic, shot on a medium-format camera, with the honest look of a real photograph. Aspect ratio 4:3
```
